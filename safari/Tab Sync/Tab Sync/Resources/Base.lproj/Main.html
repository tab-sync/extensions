Safari extension

Enable Tab Sync

Complete this one-time setup in Safari. It takes less than a minute.

3 quick steps
  1. 1

    Open the Page Menu

    Open any webpage in Safari, then tap the Page Menu button beside the address bar.

  2. 2

    Turn on Tab Sync

    Tap Manage Extensions, switch on Tab Sync, then tap Done.

    If Safari asks for website access, choose Always Allow on Every Website.

  3. 3

    Open Tab Sync

    Open the Page Menu again and tap Tab Sync. The sign-in page opens in Safari.

✓
You’re ready

Tab Sync can now keep your Safari tabs and navigation in sync.
